Request an order or delivery

Use this form to describe the materials you need and your preferred collection or delivery date. Measure the project first, then enter the quantity in the unit shown in the catalogue. For mixed orders, choose the main category and list the remaining items in the notes. A team member can confirm stock, final quantities and transport details by telephone. Delivery is arranged within Pavlodar, while customers may also collect prepared orders from Kamzina Street 199.

Before completing the form

Check product dimensions carefully. Heavy or long items may need special transport and help with unloading. Keep your phone nearby after making the request. If the order is urgent, call +7 777 132-04-74 during opening hours. The store accepts cash, card and QR payment, but payment is arranged only after the order details are confirmed.
