Order and delivery request

Use this form to describe the materials you need and your preferred collection or delivery date. Select each product from the list; the unit shown in the product name matches the catalogue. Choose collection or delivery, then fill in the address block if you need delivery within Pavlodar. Submitting the form completes your request on this page and produces a summary below.

Before completing the form

Check product dimensions carefully. Heavy or long items may need special transport and help with unloading. If the order is urgent, call +7 777 132-04-74 during opening hours. The store accepts cash, card and QR payment; payment is arranged after stock and transport details are confirmed.
